Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 5, 2026

SyncWave ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information SyncWave collects, how it is used, and your rights regarding that information.

Information We Collect

SyncWave collects only the minimum information necessary to provide its features:

  • Location data — Used by the FairwayCaddie golf feature to find nearby golf courses and show your distance to the pin. Location is only accessed while the app is in use and is never stored on our servers or shared with third parties.
  • Room codes — Temporary session codes used to sync music between devices. These are stored only in memory for the duration of your session and automatically deleted when the session ends.
  • Spotify account — If you choose to use Spotify sync, SyncWave connects to Spotify using OAuth. We do not store your Spotify credentials. Your Spotify access token is stored locally on your device only.

Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not create user accounts or collect names, email addresses, or any personal identifiers.
  • We do not track your listening history.
  • We do not use analytics or advertising SDKs.
  • We do not sell or share any data with third parties.

Location Data

Location access is used exclusively within the FairwayCaddie feature to:

  • Find golf courses near your current location using the OpenStreetMap Overpass API (a free, public service).
  • Calculate your distance to the pin during a round.

Your precise location is never transmitted to our servers. Overpass API queries are made with your approximate coordinates solely to return nearby course data.

Data Storage

SyncWave stores minimal data locally on your device (such as your current round scorecard and Spotify session token) using standard iOS app storage. This data never leaves your device except as described above.

Our sync server temporarily holds active room session state (what song is playing, current hole number) in memory only. This data is automatically purged when a session ends or after 10 minutes of inactivity.

Third-Party Services

  • Spotify — If used, subject to Spotify's Privacy Policy.
  • OpenStreetMap / Overpass API — Used for golf course search. Subject to OpenStreetMap's Privacy Policy.
  • Radio Browser API — Used for radio station data. No personal data is sent.

Children's Privacy

SyncWave is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
